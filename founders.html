01 · Trigger conditions

Find the real constraint.

01

Roadmap and architecture diverge

Product commitments depend on technical decisions nobody has made explicit.

02

Busy does not mean predictable

The team is active, but delivery, ownership and review remain unclear.

03

Pilot work cannot become a product

AI scope is larger than the evidence, workflow or operating model can support.

04

Commercial commitments are blocked

Engineering risks, integration questions or unclear ownership make the next promise unsafe.

05

Decisions keep reopening

Architecture, product and team trade-offs are revisited without a shared decision map.

06

The next boundary is unclear

There is no agreed sequence for what to fix, test, defer or own next.

02 · Diagnostic process

Turn complexity into a plan.

  1. 01Clarify the product decision and operating constraint
  2. 02Review product scope, architecture, information and ownership
  3. 03Prioritise risks by consequence and reversibility
  4. 04Agree the next cadence, decision owners and checks
