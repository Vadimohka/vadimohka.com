Current mandate & responsibilities +

I’m Vadim Vladymtsev, CTO at StackLevel Group. I work across product, architecture and engineering to help enterprise AI initiatives move from use-case selection to governed production.

Enterprise AI becomes useful only when the product, data access, controls, ownership and delivery system work together. My focus is the operating layer around the model: what the system is allowed to do, how it is evaluated, how users adopt it, and how it remains observable in production.
