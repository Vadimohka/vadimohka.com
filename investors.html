01 · Decision

Look beyond the demo.

The assessment asks whether the product solves a real workflow, whether the data position is defensible, whether the architecture and team can support the claims, and what remains unproven before a decision.

Product reality

What exists beyond the demo, and which workflow is actually supported?

Data and models

Are data rights, feedback loops, model choices and RAG quality clear?

Architecture

Can integrations, security, governance and observability survive deployment?

Team execution

Can the team make trade-offs, expose technical debt and ship the next boundary?

02 · Assessment dimensions

Ask the harder questions.

Deployment readiness

What changes when the product enters a customer's real environment?

Economics and observability

What is measured, what is expensive, and what can the team see in operation?

Open questions

Which product, data, security or delivery statements still need evidence?

Examples here are assessment questions. They are not claims about a completed review, a portfolio or a transaction.

03 · Process

A focused review.

  1. 01Management and technical interviews
  2. 02Architecture and repository review when permitted
  3. 03Product material and deployment-context review
  4. 04Risk register with red flags and open questions
  5. 05Written technical memo and decision briefing

04 · Deliverables

Evidence. Not assumptions.

01

Review summary

What was looked at, what was supplied, and which questions remain open.

02

Technical memo

Architecture, data, deployment, observability, team execution and hidden technical debt.

03

Decision briefing

Red flags, open questions, limitations and the next technical checks to run.

05 · Boundaries

A technical view. A better-informed decision.

This work is a technical assessment of product, data, architecture, deployment and team claims. It is not legal, financial, accounting or regulated investment advice, and it does not allocate capital, recommend a transaction or guarantee an outcome.

AI technical due diligence inquiries from investors and investment teams in the United States and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) are welcome.
