## Goal

Make the first 60 seconds answer who, why it matters, proof, relevance and next action.

## Why this task exists

- The current hero identifies the person and field quickly, but its credential row mostly contains roles, sectors and reach rather than outcomes.
- The homepage currently reaches background and audience segmentation before establishing enough verified authority.

## Primary target files

- `index.html`
- `assets/site.css`
- `assets/site.js`
- `sources.html`

## Implementation requirements

- Implement the narrative `Identity → Authority → Proof → Relevance → Depth → Trust → Action`.
- Use the safe H1 and supporting statement from `copy/homepage-v1.md`, adapting only for verified owner inputs.
- Use primary CTA `Discuss an enterprise AI decision` and secondary CTA `Review verified track record`.
- Limit the hero authority strip to no more than three items; include verified StackLevel role and Century product recognition.
- Create a Selected Outcomes section with only real records. It may contain fewer than three cards.
- Make Century the primary product-depth module and link its recognition source.
- Group relevance around regulated organisations, investors and founders/executives; keep speaking secondary.
- Add selected verified public work and a short biography bridge.
- Replace generic slogans with decision/outcome language.
- Preserve the current visual identity and responsive quality; do not redesign unrelated components.

## Non-goals

- No fabricated case cards.
- No framework migration.
- No technical mobile-menu rewrite unless a minimal adjustment is necessary; T02 owns the full navigation work.

## Acceptance criteria

- The first screen states professional territory, current level and a functional next action.
- Every hero proof item maps to a verified or narrowly owner-confirmed claim.
- The page sequence matches the required narrative.
- No philosophy card is presented as an outcome.
- At 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px, content remains readable with no document overflow.
- `node qa/check.mjs` passes.

## Verification

- Use the question test in `context/brand-strategy.md` against the rendered first screen.
- Capture desktop and mobile screenshots.
- Check all CTA destinations and source links.
- Review every number and role against the claim ledger.
