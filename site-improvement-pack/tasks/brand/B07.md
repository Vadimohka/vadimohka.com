## Goal

Replace generic product-velocity language with clear trigger conditions, deliverables and boundaries.

## Why this task exists

- The strongest defensible founder relevance is product, architecture and engineering diagnosis.
- The site does not substantiate fundraising, growth, exits, investment or introduction authority.

## Implementation requirements

- Use a concrete H1 about product and engineering divergence.
- Describe trigger conditions a founder recognises.
- Define the diagnostic process and deliverables: decision map, risk priorities, architecture/product diagnosis and 30/60/90-day operating plan.
- Show builder evidence through Century or another verified case, with exact personal scope.
- Make clear what the offer does not include.
- Use CTA `Request a CTO diagnostic`.
- Remove generic `technical chaos to product velocity` promises unless followed by a real case and outcome.
- Keep the tone peer-to-peer and practical rather than motivational.

## Non-goals

- No fundraising advice.
- No promises of introductions.
- No growth or exit claims.

## Acceptance criteria

- A founder can identify when to engage, what happens, what is delivered and what is outside scope.
- At least one builder proof is linked.
- No unsupported founder-success metric appears.
- `node qa/check.mjs` passes.
