## Goal

Make every site surface communicate one defensible primary identity and remove overclaiming.

## Why this task exists

- Current and legacy surfaces use several equal identities: CTO, Co-Founder, Head of R&D, AI Platform Architect, RAG Expert and sector labels.
- A strong personal brand needs a master identity, not a pile of titles.

- `*.html`
- `ai-profile.md`
- `llms.txt`

- `data/entity-graph.json`
- `sources.html`

## Implementation requirements

- Use `Enterprise AI CTO and product operator` as the default primary identity.
- Use `focused on governed private AI for regulated organisations` as the professional territory.
- Keep `CTO at StackLevel Group` as a verified current role.
- Use the Century role only if the exact owner-confirmed title and dates are available; otherwise describe Century as a product anchor without assigning an unverified title.
- Replace or narrow vague language including `serious environments`, `high stakes`, `trusted`, `real AI`, and broad bank/fintech/investment-company claims.
- Remove investor, board-level, global-leader, established thought-leader and capital-allocation implications.
- Remove or precisely specify MIPT; no vague affiliation remains.
- Keep teaching, coaching, Codeforces and certificates as supporting background, not hero authority.
- Apply the same role hierarchy in visible copy, metadata, JSON-LD and AI-readable files.
- Record every changed claim ID and source ID.

## Non-goals

- No complete page-layout rebuild.
- No external profile changes; those are handled in B11.
- No invented owner inputs.

## Acceptance criteria

- A repository-wide search finds one coherent primary professional identity.
- The verified StackLevel title is consistent on every current-role surface.
- No unsupported primary identity remains.
- No vague MIPT wording remains.
- Company-level client/metric evidence is not attributed personally.
- Visible and machine-readable profiles agree.
- `node qa/check.mjs` passes.

## Verification

- Parse all JSON-LD and entity JSON.
- Compare bios against `copy/bio-variants.md`.
