## Goal

Replace source TODOs and unsupported signal cards with exact, dated, linked records.

## Why this task exists

- `expert.html` previously contained four public-signal cards with comments indicating that verified URLs were still needed.
- Institutional readers treat an unlinked media or speaking claim as a credibility risk.

## Implementation requirements

- Replace the New AI roles signal with the Finance Mail.ru article from source S003; include outlet, 11 January 2026, exact role and a direct link.
- Replace Escaping pilot hell with the Vaiti article from S005; identify it as an authored article dated 2 March 2026.
- Replace M.AI.N AI Community with S007 and optionally S008/S009; identify the event date and exact topic/role from the organiser source.
- Replace IT-Security with S010 and optional S011; use the official programme wording and do not upgrade a panel/discussion role to keynote.
- Remove all source TODO comments from production HTML.
- Make every signal card a real link or contain a clearly labelled source link.
- Add the sources to `sources.html` and the internal claim ledger.
- Synchronise machine-readable public-work statements without calling four records a global thought-leadership corpus.
- Use external-link semantics consistently and ensure accessible link names identify the article/event.

## Non-goals

- No new fabricated media cards.
- No redesign of the whole Expert page.
- No duplicate counting of announcement and recap as separate appearances.

## Acceptance criteria

- All four initial public signals have working URLs, exact dates and accurate role labels.
- No production file contains the old verified-URL TODO comments.
- No card says keynote, award, interview or author unless the source supports that exact relation.
- Source and claim ledgers are updated.
- `node qa/check.mjs` passes and an external-link check records successful HTTP responses where network access is available.

- Search production files for `TODO`, `verified article URL`, `vaiti.io author/article`, `event post URL`, and `event page URL`.
