## Goal

Explain what Century is, what is live, what Vadim owns and what can be independently verified.

## Why this task exists

- Century is the strongest concrete product anchor but the current site presents more product philosophy than operational evidence.
- The official product site verifies capabilities; the Hi-Tech Park source verifies product recognition, not personal award.

## Implementation requirements

- Re-check the current Century product site and use only currently supported capabilities.
- Explain the enterprise problem, product surface, deployment model, governance, observability and integration boundaries.
- State Vadim's exact role only after owner confirmation; otherwise use neutral product relationship wording.
- Add the verified IT-Akademgrad 2025 product recognition with direct source.
- Add an architecture diagram, product screenshot or redacted artefact only when approved and accurate.
- Add deployment/adoption outcomes only with exact owner evidence.
- Distinguish live, beta, planned and conceptual capabilities.
- Connect Century to the personal positioning without making every company achievement personal.
- Use a functional CTA to the current Century product site or contact route.

## Non-goals

- No personal award claim.
- No unsupported client or user count.
- No speculative roadmap presented as live.

## Acceptance criteria

- A technical executive can understand Century's role, boundaries and current evidence.
- Product recognition is accurately attributed.
- Vadim's role is exact or omitted.
- Machine-readable product and person relations are consistent.
- `node qa/check.mjs` passes.

## Verification

- Compare every product capability against source S021.
- Compare recognition wording against S002.
- Audit all `founder`, `co-founder`, `creator`, `CTO` and `Century` occurrences.

node qa/check.mjs
