## Goal

Replace a mixed evidence ledger with a page that distinguishes roles, outcomes, recognition and background.

## Why this task exists

- The existing Proof page visually treats responsibilities, philosophy, teaching metrics, certificates and public work as comparable evidence.
- Institutional audiences need personal mandate, date, scale, outcome and verification.

## Implementation requirements

- Keep `proof.html` as the stage-one URL but change page title, H1 and navigation label to Track Record.
- Create sections for Current Verified Roles, Selected Outcomes, Product Recognition, Verified Public Work, and Technical/Education Foundation.
- Use the record format in `copy/case-study-template.md` for every outcome.
- Add date, role, source and scope to each proof item.
- Remove or narrow 700+, 4,700+ and 200+ metrics until the owner supplies reproducible evidence.
- Move the Claude certificate to a secondary credentials list or remove it from top-level proof.
- Remove MIPT unless exact credential evidence is supplied.
- Make source links visible and descriptive.
- State methodology: company-level sources are not personal attribution.
- Update machine-readable summary so `proof` is not described more strongly than the page.

## Non-goals

- No invented anonymised case.
- No client logo wall without personal mandate and permission.
- No URL migration.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every visible proof item has a date/range, category, exact scope and source or explicit owner-confirmed status.
- Responsibilities and beliefs are not labelled as proof.
- No unsupported aggregate metric appears.
- At least one independently verified current role, one product recognition record and one public-work record are present.
- Track Record wording is consistent in navigation and metadata.
- `node qa/check.mjs` passes.

## Verification

- Check that all external proof links resolve.
