## Goal

Bring the visual system closer to WCAG AA without losing the executive visual identity.

## Why this task exists

- Some secondary text uses opacity levels likely below 4.5:1, and meaningful labels shrink to 9–10 px on compact mobile.
- The existing focus and reduced-motion baseline is useful but incomplete.

## Implementation requirements

- Measure foreground/background contrast for all meaningful text states and meet WCAG AA targets.
- Increase meaningful labels to a target of at least 12 px; do not keep 9–10 px body-relevant text.
- Reduce extreme letter spacing where it harms mobile readability.
- Ensure focus indicators are visible against every background and not clipped.
- Mark purely decorative repeated marquee text as decorative or ensure it is meaningful and accessible.
- Apply reduced-motion behavior to reveal transitions and any new menu animation, not only the marquee/cursor light.
- Verify link underlines or another non-colour affordance for inline links.
- Ensure touch targets are reasonably sized and spaced.
- Check content at 200% browser zoom.

## Non-goals

- No palette replacement.
- No custom font introduction.
- No claim of formal WCAG certification.

## Acceptance criteria

- Normal meaningful text meets at least 4.5:1 and large text 3:1.
- No meaningful text renders below 11 px; target 12 px.
- Visible focus works on all controls.
- Reduced-motion users do not receive nonessential transitions.
- Content remains usable at 200% zoom and 320 px.
- `node qa/check.mjs` passes.

## Verification

- Run axe or equivalent automated scan.
- Record manual contrast calculations for custom opacity text.
- Run keyboard/focus review in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit when available.
