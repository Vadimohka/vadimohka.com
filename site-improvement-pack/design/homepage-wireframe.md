## Mobile hierarchy

1. Header and explicit menu button.
2. Eyebrow.
3. H1.
4. Supporting statement.
5. Primary CTA.
6. Secondary CTA.
7. Two verified authority items.
8. Portrait/product visual.
9. Outcomes.

Do not stack four or more credential cards before the primary product proof.

## Visual rule

Different evidence strengths should not look equal:

- verified outcome: strongest card;
- current role: concise authority chip;
- publication: smaller linked record;
- technical background: secondary timeline;
- planned content: never styled as published.
