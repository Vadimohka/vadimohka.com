## Editorial requirements

Each article should contain:

- executive summary;
- explicit audience;
- decision or problem;
- original framework;
- diagrams/tables where useful;
- evidence and citations;
- limitations;
- practical next step;
- Article schema;
- author and updated date.

## Publication order

Start with 1, 2, 3 and 9. They directly reinforce the primary brand territory.
