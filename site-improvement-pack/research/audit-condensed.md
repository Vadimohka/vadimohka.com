## Main diagnosis

> The site sells level before it proves scale.

## Three strongest elements

1. Clear enterprise AI territory.
2. Century as a concrete product anchor.
3. Strong static, visual and technical baseline.

## Three weakest elements

1. Claims exceed published proof.
2. Too few measurable personal outcomes.
3. Incomplete external and machine-readable identity consistency.

## Current professional perception

Most defensible:

- enterprise AI CTO/operator;
- technical founder/product builder;
- architecture and deployment specialist;
- educator with a strong technical foundation.

Not yet sufficiently evidenced:

- investor;
- capital allocator;
- board-level authority;
- global industry leader;
- established thought leader;
- fintech infrastructure veteran.

## Highest-leverage change

Create a verified Track Record using:

> Context → Personal mandate → Scale → Decision/action → Measurable outcome → Independent verification

## Brand-first reason

A technical redesign performed before evidence repair would make unsupported claims look more polished without making them more credible.
