# Owner questionnaire for real proof

Use these questions to replace generic claims with publishable evidence.

## Current roles

1. What is the exact legal/public title at StackLevel Group?
2. What date did the role begin?
3. What is the exact role in Century: founder, co-founder, creator, CTO, product lead, or another title?
4. Is Century a product, brand, company, or business unit?
5. Which current roles may be published without qualification?

## Century

1. What decisions are personally owned?
2. What product surface was personally led?
3. What team range was involved?
4. What deployment models are live rather than planned?
5. What metrics can be published: deployments, workflows, users, time-to-production, latency, adoption, cost, or quality?
6. What product claims on the current Century site are still accurate?
7. Which clients or sectors can be named?
8. Can a redacted architecture diagram or screenshot be published?
9. Who can provide an attributable reference?
