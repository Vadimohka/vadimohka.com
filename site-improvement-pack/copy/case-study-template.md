# Case study template

A strong case study is shorter than a vague success story and more specific than a list of responsibilities.

## Header

**[Decision or outcome] for [organisation type]**

- Date:
- Sector:
- Organisation display: named / anonymised
- Vadim's role:
- Source or verification:
- Confidentiality boundary:

## Context

What decision, operating problem or risk existed?

Do not begin with “The client wanted to innovate.”

## Personal mandate

What was Vadim personally responsible for deciding, leading or delivering?

Separate:

- direct ownership;
- team contribution;
- company contribution;
- third-party work.

## Constraints

Examples:

- regulated data;
- private deployment;
- legacy integration;
- latency;
- source traceability;
- model risk;
- timeline;
- procurement;
- user adoption;
- team capacity.

Only include constraints that were actually present.

## Work

What changed in:

- product scope;
- architecture;
- data access;
- governance;
- evaluation;
- team operating model;
- delivery sequence;
- ownership.

## Scale

Use verified ranges or omit:

- team;
- users;
- workflows;
- data sources;
- geography;
- duration;
- integrations;
- models.

## Outcome

Use a measurable or independently verifiable result:

- time;
- adoption;
- quality;
- cost;
- reliability;
- auditability;
- deployment;
- decision changed;
- risk discovered;
- product recognition.

Do not use “improved”, “accelerated” or “transformed” without a defined before/after.

## Verification

Provide:

- primary source;
- approved quote;
- product link;
- event/award;
- redacted artefact;
- owner confirmation.

## What cannot be disclosed

A one-sentence confidentiality note is acceptable. It must not replace all useful detail.

## CTA

Link to the most relevant engagement route, not a generic Contact button.
