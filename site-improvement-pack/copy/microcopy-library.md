- Include your organisation, current constraint, decision timeline and stakeholders.

## Honest empty-state copy

When cases are not publishable:

> Selected commercial work is confidential. Public records below show current roles, product recognition and verified public work. Additional context can be discussed under an appropriate confidentiality arrangement.

Use only if true. This sentence does not justify vague outcome claims.

## Boundary copy

### AI diligence

> Technical assessment only. This work is not legal, financial, accounting or regulated investment advice.

### Contact

> Please do not send confidential or sensitive data in an initial message.

### Confidential case

> Organisation details are withheld. The scope and outcome shown here have been approved for publication.
