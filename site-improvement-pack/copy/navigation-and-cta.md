## CTA wording rules

Prefer a verb and a concrete object:

- Review verified track record.
- Explore Century.
- Read the diligence framework.
- Request a CTO diagnostic.
- View the event programme.
- Read the article.
- Download executive bio.

Avoid:

- Learn more;
- Discover;
- Get started;
- Discuss a serious challenge;
- Let's connect;
- Unlock potential.

## Contact fallback

Until a direct email or endpoint is owner-approved:

- keep LinkedIn as the functional primary route;
- show clear inquiry categories;
- do not render fake submit controls;
- do not invent a response-time promise.
