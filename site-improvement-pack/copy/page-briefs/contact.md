# Page brief — Contact

## Job

Convert a qualified visitor without forcing them to infer how to reach Vadim.

## H1

Start with the decision that needs to be made.

## Inquiry routes

- Enterprise deployment.
- AI technical diligence.
- CTO/founder diagnostic.
- Executive briefing.
- Media comment.
- Speaking invitation.

## Functional requirements

- Direct professional email when owner-approved.
- LinkedIn fallback.
- Form only with a real endpoint.
- No sensitive data requested initially.
- Privacy/processing note when a form exists.
- Preserve URL query or fragment to preselect intent.

## Avoid

- “Bring a serious challenge.”
- a dead submit button;
- invented response-time promise;
- public personal email without permission.
