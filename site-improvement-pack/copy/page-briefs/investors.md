# Page brief — AI Diligence (`investors.html`)

## Job

Present a rigorous technical assessment method without implying investment authority or an unverified mandate history.

## Core question

Will the company's AI product, data, architecture, team and deployment claims survive real operating conditions?

## Assessment dimensions

- product reality;
- data rights and defensibility;
- model/RAG quality;
- architecture and integration;
- security and governance;
- deployment readiness;
- observability and economics;
- team execution;
- hidden technical debt;
- claims-to-evidence gaps.

## Deliverables

Describe a sample scope:

- management/technical interviews;
- architecture and repository review when permitted;
- product evidence review;
- risk register;
- red flags and open questions;
- written technical memo;
- decision briefing.

## Boundaries

State that this is technical assessment, not legal, financial, accounting or regulated investment advice.

## Proof gate

No completed engagement count, fund reference or transaction outcome without owner evidence.

## CTA

Request AI technical diligence.
