# Page brief — Founder Advisory

## Job

Show a founder when a CTO-level diagnostic is useful and what concrete output they receive.

## Trigger conditions

- roadmap and architecture diverge;
- team is busy but delivery remains unpredictable;
- AI scope is larger than the product evidence;
- ownership is unclear;
- pilot work cannot become a product;
- engineering decisions block commercial commitments.

## Deliverables

- decision map;
- product/architecture diagnosis;
- prioritised technical risks;
- 30/60/90-day operating plan;
- ownership and delivery cadence;
- optional architecture review.

## Boundaries

Do not promise fundraising, growth, exits, introductions or investment.

## CTA

Request a CTO diagnostic.
