# Page brief — Enterprise AI

## Job

Make a bank or enterprise executive believe the conversation will cover governance and execution, not an AI demo.

## Core message

Private enterprise AI requires product, architecture, controlled data access, evaluation, ownership, auditability and adoption to work as one operating system.

## Required sections

1. Decisions this work addresses.
2. Operating model from use case to production.
3. Governance/security controls.
4. Deliverables.
5. Relevant case/proof.
6. Clear boundaries and CTA.

## Safe language

“Designed for regulated environments” and “focus on” are safer than “trusted by banks” until personal case evidence exists.

## CTA

Scope a governed AI deployment.
