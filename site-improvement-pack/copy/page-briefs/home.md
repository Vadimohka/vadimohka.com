# Page brief — Home

## Job

Turn an unknown senior visitor into a qualified second action within 60 seconds.

## Primary audience

Banks/regulatory institutions, enterprise/FinTech executives, investors, founders.

## Narrative

Identity → Authority → Proof → Relevance → Depth → Trust → Action.

## Required modules

1. Positioning hero.
2. Verified authority strip.
3. Selected outcomes.
4. Century product anchor.
5. Audience relevance.
6. Verified public work.
7. Short biography bridge.
8. Intent-specific action.

## Proof gate

No role, sector, metric, client or outcome enters the hero unless its claim status is verified or narrowly owner-confirmed.

## Remove/reduce

- repeated “serious” language;
- four equal audience/credential cards before proof;
- generic “product taste” slogan;
- responsibilities presented as outcomes.

## CTA

Primary: Discuss an enterprise AI decision.  
Secondary: Review verified track record.
