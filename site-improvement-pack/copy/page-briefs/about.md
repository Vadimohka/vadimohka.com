# Page brief — About (`origin.html`)

## Job

Provide an exact, credible career path without allowing the education story to overshadow current operating work.

## Structure

1. Current role and professional territory.
2. Career timeline with dates.
3. Century/StackLevel mandate.
4. Engineering background.
5. BSUIR teaching/coaching.
6. Selected technical profiles/publications.
7. Downloadable bio.

## Evidence rules

- current role: official company source;
- historical BSUIR roles: official university sources;
- Codeforces: exact profile facts;
- Scholar: publication-level verification;
- MIPT: exact credential or remove.

## Tone

Factual and chronological. Avoid self-mythologising language such as “built through hard work” unless it is a short personal note after the factual timeline.
