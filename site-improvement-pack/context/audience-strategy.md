# Audience strategy

## 1. Banks and regulated financial institutions

### Decision they are making

Can this person be trusted to discuss or lead a governed enterprise AI initiative without underestimating security, compliance, operating ownership, and legacy integration?

### Evidence required

- exact current role;
- security and governance implementation detail;
- product/deployment artefact;
- personal mandate;
- permitted client or anonymised case;
- measurable production outcome;
- clarity about boundaries and risk.

### Primary CTA

**Scope a governed AI deployment**

### Avoid

- generic innovation language;
- implied bank clients without attribution;
- crypto-style urgency;
- unqualified compliance claims;
- architecture claims without deployment context.

## 2. Enterprise and FinTech executives

### Decision they are making

Does Vadim understand enough product, architecture, and execution to help move a priority AI use case beyond pilot stage?

### Evidence required

- operating framework;
- delivery scope;
- product adoption logic;
- integration and observability;
- team leadership;
- production constraints and trade-offs.

### Primary CTA

**Discuss an enterprise AI decision**

### Avoid

- presenting generic AI strategy as sector expertise;
- implying payments/core-banking depth without evidence;
- treating a demo as a deployment.

## 3. Investors

### Decision they are making

Can this operator help test whether an AI company’s product, data, architecture, team, and deployment claims will survive diligence?

### Evidence required

- transparent assessment framework;
- sample deliverable;
- scope and limitations;
- completed mandates or references, when available;
- clear separation from investment advice.

### Primary CTA

**Request AI technical diligence**

### Avoid

- “investor” as a personal role;
- capital-allocation claims;
- implied deal track record;
- guaranteed conclusions;
- hidden conflicts.

## 4. Founders

### Decision they are making

Can this person identify why product, architecture, roadmap, and engineering execution have diverged and define a credible next operating plan?

### Evidence required

- product-building experience;
- diagnosis framework;
- architecture and team operating examples;
- concrete deliverables;
- founder/client reference when available.

### Primary CTA

**Request a CTO diagnostic**

### Avoid

- fundraising, growth, exits, or introductions unless separately evidenced;
- vague “product velocity” promises;
- claiming responsibility for company-wide outcomes without scope.

## 5. C-level and boards

### Decision they are making

Can this person frame an AI decision in terms of accountability, risk, operating model, investment, adoption, and measurable outcome?

### Evidence required

- concise executive communication;
- decision framework;
- governance model;
- cross-functional stakeholder context;
- board or executive reference when available.

### Primary CTA

**Request an executive AI briefing**

### Avoid

- claiming board-level experience merely through tone or design;
- unmeasured ROI;
- one-size-fits-all AI transformation language.

## 6. Media and conferences

### Decision they are making

Is this a credible, quotable, available expert with a defined territory and a verified public record?

### Evidence required

- links to published quotes/articles;
- event programmes and recordings;
- defined speaking topics;
- short and long bio;
- downloadable approved portrait;
- direct media contact.

### Primary CTA

**Invite Vadim to speak**

Media and speaking remain a secondary authority layer, not a primary homepage offer.
